Technical Documentation Standards for 2026-27

The Saksham portal's integration with the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system has introduced new automated verification triggers. My research focuses on helping students from marginalized backgrounds and migrant families navigate these technical hurdles without losing their eligibility.

🚨 Critical Correction for OBC/Migrant Applicants:

A significant number of rejections occur because of the "Date Conflict" rule. For the 2026 session, all Central OBC-NCL certificates must be timestamped after April 1st, 2026. Furthermore, students who have migrated from states like Jharkhand must provide a verified Vanshavali to link their current residency to their ancestral caste status.
